#### Who is buying what? (Interesting Patterns)
The data reveals two strong, non-obvious demographic trends:
- **Teenagers are driving Accessory sales:** Teenage girls are heavily purchasing items in the Accessories section (like belts). We should strategically place high-margin, low-cost accessories near the checkout counter to capitalize on their impulse buying habits.
- **Adult Men prefer plain Bottoms:** Adult men (31-45 years) are almost exclusively purchasing basic, plain-colored bottoms (like Navy Blue or Black jeans). We must ensure the Men's section is always well-stocked with these core basics, as they represent reliable, recurring revenue from this demographic.

### What to Avoid 🚫
- **Avoid ordering seasonal mismatch items:** Stop ordering Winter Beanies in non-winter months. Even with steep discounts, they sit on the shelf and take up valuable space.
- **Avoid high quantities of XS sizes:** Stop applying a "one-size-fits-all" ordering strategy. We are ordering way too many XS units compared to actual demand.
- **Avoid extreme fashion trends:** The Neon Green Cargo Pants failed completely. Our core customers prefer basic colors, so we should avoid risking budget on loud, experimental styles.
